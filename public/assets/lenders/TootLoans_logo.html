Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow?

You can apply for a loan of between £1,000 and £5,000. We will assess your application to ensure that your monthly payments are affordable.

What can I use my personal loan for?

You could use your personal loan for a variety of purposes including:

One off large purchases

You can spread the cost of those one off large purchases over a longer term making them more affordable.

Buying a car

Whether you're buying your first car or upgrading to a newer model, a loan gives you the freedom to buy your car exactly where you want, with no limit to the miles you can do. Loans are not secured against your car.

Home improvement

A personal loan could bring your home improvement plans to life. From an extra bathroom, a new kitchen, to a loft conversion, you could borrow the money you need to make the change you want.

Special occasion

Dream wedding? If you have an occasion you need to fund, a personal loan could help you make your plans a reality.

Unacceptable loan purposes

We are unable to lend for certain purposes such as consolidating existing debts.

What happens when I apply?

We make use of the Open Banking process to assess your income and expenditure to ensure that the loan we provide is affordable to you. The process is regulated by the FCA and enables you to share your financial data with a loan provider such as Toot Loans in a safe and secure environment.

The open banking information is provided by oohMoolah. They cannot provide your financial information without your express consent. oohMoolah is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and has access to all of the main UK banks and building societies

The Open Banking Process

  • Once you have completed the Toot loans application form and pressed the submit button you will be automatically directed to the ooMoolah website.
  • OoMoolah will request your bank account details, including sort code and account number.
  • You will be required to provide permission for ooMoolah to access your financial information.
  • You will then be automatically directed back to the Toot Loans website to receive your lending decision.

This ensures that the decision process is quick and accurate. It also means that you are not required to provide any other form of evidence regarding income and expenditure.

It is both safe and secure and has no impact your credit score.

How do I qualify for a Toot loan?

To qualify you must be:

  • Aged 23 or over
  • A UK resident
  • Lived at your current address for at least 1 year
  • Employed with a net monthly income of £1,000 or more

Ready to get started?

Before considering a personal loan, you should take some time to work out whether a loan is your best option. You should never take out a loan if you’re worried about whether you can meet the repayments.
